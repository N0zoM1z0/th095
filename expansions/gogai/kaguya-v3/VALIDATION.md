fingerprints derived from actual origins, trajectories and schedules, excluding

a finite rectangle/diagonal cage, orbiting curling fire, and

The fountain has 2584 sampled same-slot/tag trajectories with an
observed upward velocity followed by downward velocity. The returning card has
1264 sampled velocity reversals after its turn phase. Speeds
are measured from velocity magnitude, rather than the scalar speed field, which
has different semantics under vector acceleration.

The final card reaches 401 simultaneously active tagged native
children. 4136 sampled child appearances at stateTimer age at

observed separation is 13.29 units. This is asynchronous
near-origin evidence; the native packet's position assignment is the separate
source contract. No scene reaches the 1,600-slot native capacity.

Direct-birth observations use unseen slot/tag pairs at stateTimer age at most
four ticks. They are measured from the actual published source, not from the
actor when a projector is remote. All eight cards have samples within the
64-unit observation bound. Exact source equality is separately checked in the
model. Real casting frames 10-13 have 1948 sampled states and
1 asynchronous mismatch. This uses existing original art.
