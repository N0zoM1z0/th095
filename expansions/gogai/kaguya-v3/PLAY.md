# Kaguya: Eight Different Wonders / Hard v3

Open PREVIEW.html to compare all eight mechanisms and their formation samples.

Double-click launch-gogai.bat. Choose Game Start, then press Left once from
LEVEL 1 to reach Extra Issue 1 (group 12). All eight scenes are available.

Arrow keys move; Shift focuses. Shift+Z charges quickly. At full charge,
release Shift and hold Z to aim; release Z to photograph Kaguya. Escape pauses.
Scenes 1-5 require six valid photos, scenes 6-7 seven, and scene 8 eight.

Projectors are visible firing sources, not extra photo targets. Watch them
travel into position before they fire. The seven-color relay, laser cage,
rotating fire barrier, aimed eyes, upward fountains, crossing gates, returning
shells and splitting seeds have different movement demands.

Bait the dragon's warning before reframing. Read both the rising and falling
halves of the fountains. Returning shells cross their earlier routes. A seed
can be photographed before it flowers; native children are also photographable.
Lasers extend through a thin warning before collision growth. Native photography
can cut their geometry.

Run run-no-death-test.bat for no-death practice. Keep its PowerShell helper
beside it. It accepts only this artifact's verified hash and death prologue,
patches one byte in its newly started process and verifies the write. No disk
executable is changed. The time limit remains active. Close that process and
use launch-gogai.bat for ordinary play. Replays containing collisions require
no-death playback.

Manual launches retain normal windowed graphics, music and sound. This folder
has fresh independent scores, photos and replays. Do not mix earlier issue saves
into it. Earlier Kaguya and Sakuya folders remain preserved. See SPELL_LIBRARY.md
for source versions and VALIDATION.md for actual coverage and remaining limits.
Human balance and artistic preference remain for playtesting. This is original
fan content, not unreleased ZUN content.
