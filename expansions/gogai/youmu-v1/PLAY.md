# Youmu: Eight Sword Scenes / Hard v1

Open PREVIEW.html to compare the eight sword mechanisms and their real formation
samples. Run launch-gogai.bat for normal play. Choose Game Start, then press
Left once from LEVEL 1 to reach Extra Issue 1 (group 12). All eight slots feature
Youmu. Scenes 1-5 require six photos, scenes 6-7 seven, and scene 8 eight.

Arrow keys move; Shift focuses. Shift+Z charges quickly. At full charge,
release Shift and hold Z to aim; release Z to photograph. Escape pauses.

Read the sheath pose and thin warning before a cut. Youmu's dash is short;
her projectile wake lasts longer. Use the follow-through to reframe a photo.
The white half-spirit is a visible firing source, not a second photo target.
Watch it separately in scenes 3, 7 and 8. Scene 4 has reflected blades. Scene 6
leaves still knives that release later; scene 7 turns its petals after they fly.
The final scene makes three distinct cuts before its closing fan.

Run run-no-death-test.bat for no-death practice and keep the PowerShell helper
beside it. It verifies this artifact's hash and death-entry prologue, patches
one byte in its own newly started process, restores protection and reads back
the write. The disk executable is unchanged and the time limit remains active.
Close that process and use launch-gogai.bat for ordinary play. Replays containing
collisions require no-death playback.

Manual launches retain normal windowed graphics, music and sound. Test configs
and diagnostic saves are not delivered. This product owns fresh separate scores,
Best Shots and replays; do not mix another issue's saves into it. Earlier Sakuya
and Kaguya releases remain preserved. See SPELL_LIBRARY.md and VALIDATION.md.
Human balance and artistic preference remain for playtesting. This is original
fan content, not unreleased ZUN content.
