# Kaguya: Eight Impossible Nights / Hard v1

Open PREVIEW.html for actual gameplay captures and an orbiting-emitter sequence.
The gallery runs without starting the game.

Double-click launch-gogai.bat. Choose Game Start, then press Left once from
LEVEL 1 to select Extra Issue 1 (group 12). All eight scenes are available.
This independent issue features Kaguya throughout. See README.md for the eight
compositions and SPELL_LIBRARY.md for the preserved Sakuya spell sets.

Arrow keys move. Shift focuses. Shift+Z charges quickly. At full charge, release
Shift and hold Z to aim; release Z to photograph. Escape pauses. Photographing
Kaguya is required for each valid shot. Orbiting ornaments are not extra subjects.
The time limit remains active in both normal and no-death modes.

Read each gathering pose before its mass release. Small fast layers overtake
large slow jewels. Some jewels wait in geometric arrangements, then accelerate
and turn in opposing directions. Aya can still move and photograph while those
jewels wait. Dragon and medley phrases also include finite wall/ceiling bounces.

Lasers have an initial thin warning, then grow into active beams. The star nest
uses bounded pentagram segments. Bamboo has crossing slanted beams. Photography
uses the native beam cutting/cancellation behavior. A charged jewel arrangement,
its release and a sustained stream can overlap; an opening is not permanent.

Double-click run-no-death-test.bat for no-death practice. Keep its PowerShell
helper in the same folder. The helper accepts only the verified Hard v1 artifact,
checks its hash and native death-function bytes, then patches one byte in its
new game process and verifies the write. The executable on disk is unchanged.
Close that process and use launch-gogai.bat for ordinary collision behavior.
Replays containing collisions require no-death playback.

Manual BAT launches include normal windowed graphics, music and effects. This
folder owns fresh scores, photographs and replays; do not copy old issue saves
into it. Earlier Sakuya standalone folders remain available independently.

These are experimental difficult fan compositions. Native diagnostic completion
and ordinary collision behavior have separate coverage in VALIDATION.md; human
balance and artistic preference remain for playtesting. This is original fan
content, not unreleased ZUN content.
